VestTrack Support

VestTrack helps you track RSU grants, project vesting income, and spot withholding shortfalls before tax season.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
Everything stays on your device. VestTrack has no account system and makes no network requests.

How do I update my stock price?
Open the grant from the Grants tab and edit "Current price". All projections update instantly.

What does "shortfall at filing" mean?
Employers withhold a flat 22% federal rate on RSU vest income, but your true marginal rate may be higher. The difference becomes a balance due when you file. VestTrack shows the gap and the W-4 Step 4(c) amount that would close it.

Are the tax figures official?
They are estimates based on published 2026 federal brackets and state rates — for planning only, not tax advice. Consult a qualified tax professional.
