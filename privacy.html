VestTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

The short version

VestTrack collects no personal data. None. The app works entirely offline: your grants, share prices, and settings are stored only on your device, and the app makes no network requests at all.

Data stored on your device

Grant details (company, share counts, grant dates, vesting structure) are saved locally in the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app.

Purchases

Any future optional upgrade would be processed by Apple through the App Store's in-app purchase system. We never see your payment information.

Third parties

VestTrack contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking technologies, and no third-party data processors.

Children

VestTrack does not collect any data from anyone, including children, and is rated 4+.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/callingmea-svg/vesttrack/issues
